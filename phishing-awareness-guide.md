# Phishing Awareness Guide

## 1. What is Phishing?
Phishing is a form of social engineering where attackers pretend to be a trusted person or company to trick you into giving your password or clicking a bad link.

## 2. 7 Signs of a Phishing Email
1. Urgency - "Your account will close in 1 hour!"
2. Wrong sender - e.g. support@paypa1.com instead of support@paypal.com
3. Generic greeting - "Dear Customer" instead of your name
4. Suspicious link - Hover over the link, it goes to a different website
5. Unexpected attachment - .exe, .zip, .html file you did not ask for
6. Spelling mistakes and poor grammar
7. Asks for password or card details - Real companies never ask for this by email

## 3. Types of Phishing
- Email phishing - Fake email to many people
- Spear phishing - Targeted email to one person
- Smishing - Phishing by SMS
- Vishing - Phishing by phone call

## 4. What to do if you receive a phishing email
1. DO NOT click the link
2. DO NOT download the attachment
3. DO NOT reply
4. Report to IT immediately
5. Delete the email after IT says so
6. If you already clicked, change your password immediately and tell IT
