# Phishing Awareness Resource

## What is this project?
This project is a simple phishing awareness resource for employees in any organisation. It helps staff understand what phishing is, how to spot a phishing email, and what to do if they receive one.

## Who is it for?
All staff, especially non-technical staff who use email every day.

## What is inside?
- phishing-awareness-guide.md - Full guide on phishing
- phishing-checklist.md - Quick checklist before you click
- incident-reporting-template.md - Form to report a phishing email

## How to use it
1. Read the guide once.
2. Print the checklist and keep it near your computer.
3. If you get a suspicious email, do NOT click. Fill in the reporting template and send it to IT.
